Welcome to

Forest Cafe

We started in 2019 with four tables, one roaster and a slope full of coffee plants. Today the same slope feeds the kitchen, the shop and the six rooms behind the cafe. Nothing travels far here — most of it walks down the hill in the morning.

Welcome to

Four acres behind the cafe: coffee, pepper, turmeric, ginger and a few beehives we mostly leave alone. Whatever we cannot use in the kitchen gets dried, milled and packed by hand for the shop.

WELCOME TO

Our Tiny Cot

Six peaceful rooms behind the cafe, built low so the trees stay taller. Warm beds, hot water, thick blankets, and wide windows opening to cool morning mist. No television screens—just fresh air, birdsong, and open space for little ones to play freely.

"Breakfast is whatever came fresh off the farm that morning — served warm, healthy, and kid-approved."
